# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.7 player checks

Install the [signed 0.1.7 debug APK](../builds/GardenBadBloom-0.1.7-debug.apk) when you finish the current playtest. Resume keeps the active floor layout and the health of enemies already spawned. To compare the new balance from the first room, finish or end the current run before starting a new one.

## Combat and music

- After the first boss, check that Angela’s portrait stays on screen while you read, choose, and confirm a talent. **Camp Blessing** shows its +1 run damage and full class meter before you choose. The next chapter waits for confirmation.
- With Angela, activate her shield without absorbing a hit, then check its one-second recharge. Absorb a hit from the side or rear and check the five-second recharge. A frontal hit in the opening window should still earn perfect-guard credit for her evolution.
- With each heroine, attack while standing and while changing direction. Use the skill and look for a distinct windup, release, and recovery. Check whether the held button still gives you the expected aim preview on Vivianne and Virelia.
- Try a fresh Forgotten Garden and Mushroom Kingdom clear. Check the regular enemy pressure, the time to bring bosses down, and whether the phase-two patterns give you enough time to react. Look for the thrown crown, enemy projectiles, roots, clock hands, teacups, and falling stars in their respective biomes.
- Kill His Moldy Majesty and watch the crown fly loose before the boss chest becomes available. If you interrupt the game during the animation, continue once and check that the single chest still appears. Any living attendants still have to be cleared.
- Listen for the flute reel in Forgotten Garden, the reed tune in Mushroom Kingdom, and the clockwork rhythm. The main title track is the same recording as 0.1.6.

## Rooms and Garden

- Visit the optional paths. Open **Cap Inspection** and **The Moldy Crown** when offered. Their artwork should show a green cap with a loupe and a mossy gold crown respectively.
- On a newly generated Mushroom floor, look for **Three Caps in a Row** on a branch. It costs 6 Petals to enter. A draw returns the stake. Winning gives a Petal Bomb or +1 run damage if your pocket is filled. Resume during the keeper’s turn to check that the board and turn persist.
- At a curse altar, check that **Cursed Lily** produces two separately animated basic hits or projectiles. **Thorn Tithe** should show that it costs 4 HP per combat room and grant 25% more hit damage.
- Compare healing and Petal spending across one full floor. Camps offer up to 18 HP, shops appear only on branches, and most regular chests offer class meter in place of tea.
- Watch the title portraits rotate, then enter the Garden and tap the walking heroines to see their emotes. Seed Shop offers span starting items, five weapon licenses, and courtyard decorations. A weapon already found in the dungeon should appear ready without spending Seeds.
