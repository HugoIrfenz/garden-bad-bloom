# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.10 player checks

## Find Three Caps in a Row

- Reach a Mushroom Kingdom floor generated in 0.1.7 or later. Look for a board on the minimap and choose the marked side gate when it becomes available. The normal route continues alongside it.
- Approach the capkeeper’s board and choose **Play Three Caps** with at least 6 Petals. Winning gives a Petal Bomb or +1 run damage if your pocket is full. A draw refunds the entry stake.
- A Mushroom floor saved before the event was added keeps its original map. A newly generated Mushroom floor includes the capkeeper branch.

## Boss health

- Check the Gardener’s full health bar. Newly spawned campaign Gardener encounters range from 1,200 to 1,295 HP, depending on the floor’s room count. His Moldy Majesty ranges from 1,390 to 1,490 HP.
- Resume an older save during a live boss fight. The current and maximum HP should both be five times their saved values, with the same percentage of health remaining. Leave and resume the same fight again to confirm that the scale applies once.
- The multiplier applies to campaign bosses and Garden Without End bosses. Check that phase transitions and the reward still occur when expected. Majesty’s two attendants keep their existing health scale.
