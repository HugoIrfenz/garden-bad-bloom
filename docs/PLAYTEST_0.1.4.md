# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.4 player checks

This is an interim build of the full base-game project. Garden 0.1.4 introduces the first equipment, talent, curse, and permanent-unlock mechanics. Gameplay, acceptance, and any headless test execution are the owner's.

## High-value checks on the Xiaomi

1. **Weapon and resume.** Start any heroine and reach the first Treasure node. The third reward card shows a compatible weapon and compares its base damage and attack interval. Restart with that reward open and confirm the offer is the same. Equip it, check its name in Pause, then use it in the next fight. The Weapon Ledger is under Enemy Codex. The discovery adds a Swap button to that heroine’s Run Preparation card on a subsequent run.
2. **Merchant and relic.** A merchant now offers five tiles. Tap the weapon to compare, then go back or buy. Compare before and after a restart to check the stock and Petals balance. Honest Receipt can appear in a chapter boss chest. Buy something on one merchant visit and check that the first eligible purchase at a different merchant applies a 5-Petal credit. With Royal Cleaver equipped, the receipt also primes an extra guard-counter hit for the next two rooms.
3. **First talent.** Clear the Forgotten Garden boss, take the chest reward, and walk through the chapter exit. The talent screen should open once. Restart on that screen and choose Learn Talent or continue without it. Check that the selected choice opens Mushroom Kingdom and that a learned talent changes the matching combat action.
4. **Curses and Bloodbloom.** At a curse altar, read the named benefit and drawback before committing. Check the active curse in Pause and its damage, merchant or healing effect. Bloodbloom’s new events include illustrated safe and risky options. The fox, lantern and spider-thread options can turn an event into a playable encounter.
5. **Persistent Garden.** Open the Seed Shop and Run History from the hub. Purchase an unlock once and restart. Confirm the Seed balance and unlock persist. An unlocked pocket item can be selected in Run Preparation. The lanterns should light the Garden.
6. **Language.** Switch English or Bahasa Indonesia in Settings and restart. Check title, Garden, Settings, Seed Shop and Run History. Other screens still contain English strings in this build.

The goal of this build is to check the new mechanics inside the existing offline run. The remaining base-game catalogs are tracked in the [release ledger](RELEASE_LEDGER.md).
