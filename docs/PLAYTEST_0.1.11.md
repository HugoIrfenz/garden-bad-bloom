# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.11 player checks

From the title, open **Garden → Garden Games**. All five games are available in the pavilion for free practice. Dungeon versions appear on marked side routes when those floors are generated. A save keeps any floor it has already generated.

| Game | Dungeon | Controls and goal | Entry and win |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Petal Prix | Forgotten Garden | Alternate the two pedals after the countdown. Fast stretches trigger flower boosts and puddles slow unboosted karts. Beat the Capkeeper to the finish. | 6 Petals → 15 Petals |
| Three Caps in a Row | Mushroom Kingdom | Tap an empty tile and make a line of three before the keeper does. | 6 Petals → Petal Bomb, or +1 damage if your pocket is full |
| Clockwork Encore | Clockwork Conservatory | Watch the organ light up, then replay the four keys in the same order. Clear three verses of 3, 4, and 6 notes. You get one retry. | 5 Petals → 8 Petals and up to 2 class pips |
| Bramble Snake | Bloodbloom Forest | Use the four direction buttons to collect 6 petals before time runs out. Avoid the hedge and your own tail. | 5 Petals → Petal Bomb, or 8 Petals if your pocket is full |
| Teacup Catch | Hollow Palace | Tap lanes to move the tray. Catch 8 good cups and avoid cracked ones. Three spills end the round. | 5 Petals → Clockwise Tonic, or 9 Petals if your pocket is full |

- Try all five under Garden Games. Finish or forfeit a practice round and confirm your current expedition and currency remain as they were.
- Find Petal Prix on a newly generated Forgotten Garden floor. Follow the marked fork and start the race. Interrupt after a boost or puddle, then Continue and check that race distance and the remaining stake carry through.
- Test a win and claim the prize once. A draw refunds the entry, and a loss or forfeit spends it. Pause during a round to check that time and controls resume from the same point.
- In Run Preparation, compare the starting relic effects and triggers. At a chest, tap **Inspect Relic**, read its limits and pairings, then choose **Back to Rewards** or **Equip Relic**. Back keeps the chest open.
