# Storybook prologue

Hugo & Angela Works leads into five illustrated pages, followed by the title screen. The pages use the supplied English narration exactly. Each page has its own arrangement of the title melody and a timed effect cue. [Prologue audio](AUDIO.md#storybook-prologue)

## Player checks

- Launch the app from a fresh start. The studio marks should give way to The Creator. The five pages advance in order over about 35 seconds, ending at the title.
- Look for water at the can’s spout, the five empty blossoms dissolving into the five heroines, sunlight and tea at the picnic, the spread of the crimson blight, and the weapon glints at the gate. Listen for the score and individual sound accents.
- Tap the page to advance or **Skip intro** to go directly to the title. On desktop, Right, Enter, or Space advances and Escape skips. A tap that shortens the studio card should still open page one.
- With music or sound effects disabled, relaunch and check the switches independently. Background the app mid-page and return to check that the story continues from the same point.

## Illustrations and prompts

The built-in imagegen tool used the owner’s panel as the style reference. Godot supplies the narration, framed layout, motion, and transitions. The art files are in [the prologue asset folder](../assets/art/prologue/).
