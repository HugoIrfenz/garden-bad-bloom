# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.5 player checks

This build adds the first two weapon evolutions. The forge appears in unspent rest rooms for Angela and Vivianne. Player-side gameplay checks cover a new run and a restart while the forge is open.

## Forge

- At a rest room, choose **Inspect weapon evolution**. Read the ingredient names and their chapter sources. Back should return to the rest choices, with healing and honing still available. A restart with the forge open should restore the same screen and ingredients.
- Forge when every requirement is checked. The new weapon replaces the old one, the ingredient relics stay in the run, and the rest room is spent. Pause should show the new weapon. The Weapon Ledger should reveal it as a dungeon evolution.
- An already open boss reward from 0.1.4 retains its saved offer. Use a fresh run for the chapter-specific source checks below.

## Angela

Take **Thornmail** from the Forgotten Garden boss chest and **Guardian’s Ledger** from the Clockwork Conservatory boss chest. Land three genuine perfect guards in the run. The forge counter should advance once per guard activation when an attack is actually blocked in the timing window. Forge **Rosewall Execution** at a later rest room.

After forging, a perfect guard should send seven thorns and prime one rose sweep. A missed swing should leave the sweep available. Restart with it primed and confirm it triggers on the next landed basic attack, then clears.

## Vivianne

Take **Rosebound Refrain** from the Mushroom Kingdom boss chest and **Black Rose Ink** from the Bloodbloom Forest boss chest. Forge **Elegy Spiral** at a later rest room. A curved shot that lands should leave one ink trace. Land a second shot or use Midnight Verse close to the first trace to form a gravity well. Check that nearby eligible enemies pull in, cover blocks the effect, and bosses stay in place. Restart with a verse echo or convergence burst pending and check it fires once.
