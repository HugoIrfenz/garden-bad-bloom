# 0.1.13 heroine balance checks

- Angela's Last Stand cut-in is faster and fires a forward blade wave. Royal Guard protects from all directions for 0.9 seconds. The skill button shows gold **Parry** for the first 0.24 seconds and blue **Guard** until the shield falls. Catch a rear projectile, a ground hazard, a root grab, and a boss shove. Try raising the shield during a dodge. Check the one-second recharge when nothing connects and the five-second recharge that starts on the first parry.
- Angela's weapons deal one more point of base damage per hit. Lilyblade deals 14, Morning Promise 13, Royal Cleaver 19, and Rosewall Execution 22.
- Vivianne's third basic attack fires three projectiles. With Cursed Lily, the repeated attack fires another three. Her Rose Lens starting relic increases basic projectile range. Vivianne walks at 302 units per second before modifiers and covers 64 units during a 0.5-second Dodge in open space.
- Nixia can use Unauthorized Access again as soon as a leap deals damage. Cast immediately to start a fresh leap, or wait for up to two automatic follow-ups on the same enemy. A leap that deals zero damage starts a three-second cooldown on landing. Orchid Spring reduces it to 2.4 seconds before other modifiers. Interrupt during a leap or chain and resume.
- Virelia's skill is now a directional dash with its own cooldown, separate from dodge. Her damage is lower and movement is faster. Camellia Ribbon adds movement speed. Snare Bloom creates a delayed ring at the skill-dash endpoint; Sewing Thread connects two skill-dash endpoints.
- Zaria's Dodge now recharges in 0.5 seconds before modifiers, and she has 65 max HP. Existing runs cap her saved HP at the new maximum. Her Graffiti Cut skill dash travels farther and has its own recharge. Iris Dice gives each damaging hit a chance to deal double damage.
- Select each signature relic in Run Preparation and confirm it remains equipped after an interruption. Check touch feel, animation, readability, damage, and performance on the phone.
- Try the floating joystick with small and full drags, mirrored controls, and simultaneous attacks. The initial adjustment uses a smaller deadzone and reaches full movement with less travel.
- In ordinary combat and challenge rooms, look for occasional larger, crowned elite enemies; every enemy in an elite room is elite. Their warnings resolve 20% faster, with a minimum warning of 0.3 seconds.
- Elite enemies have a 30% chance to drop a small heal that restores up to 8 HP when touched. A drop remains in the room after an interruption and respects healing-blocking curses.
- Curse altars can offer five new bargains: Quickthorn, Heavy Bloom, Wild Roots, Golden Bargain, and Fragile Grace. Compare their stated benefits and costs, combine them with other curses, and check the bound-curses list and interruption recovery.

Bloomheart now has five phases with higher health and new late-fight patterns. Check the first fight blind. On a repeat, interrupt during a late warning and confirm the telegraph and phase resume.

The signature relics currently reuse existing item art. Their balance values are initial estimates pending device playtesting.
