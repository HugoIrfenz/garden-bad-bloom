# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.12 player checks

## Garden purchases

- Open **Garden → Seed Shop**. The four shelves hold 70 offers. This build adds six alternate weapon licenses, eight animated Garden decorations, two machines, five game cartridges, and 37 OST tapes. New items cost 21–72 Garden Seeds.
- In **Garden Games**, tap a locked game to find the Petal Console or its cartridge on the right shop page. The console costs 58 Seeds and cartridges cost 30–46. Owning both lets you replay that game in the Garden.
- Open **Garden → Jukebox**. Buy the cabinet for 68 Seeds and any soundtrack VHS for 21–65. Check owned tapes across the story, menu, dungeon, boss, and arcade collections. Play an owned tape, pause and resume, switch repeat, then leave the jukebox. The Music setting also controls playback.
- In a newly generated campaign, the first five chapters each put their game directly on the main route as a room event. You can play there by paying the usual Petal stake or leave through the exit. Older checkpoints keep their existing floor layout.

## Relics

Open the **Relic Ledger** in the Codex or Run Preparation. It shows the source, heroine restrictions, trigger, carried or discovered status, and forge pairings for all 13 relics. The three universal starter options are available in Run Preparation. Class and forge relics are found during runs.

| Fixed source | Relic |
| --- | --- |
| Forgotten Garden treasure | Honest Receipt |
| Mushroom Kingdom treasure as Angela | Counterclock Locket |
| Clockwork Conservatory treasure | Glass Eyelash |
| Bloodbloom Forest treasure | Last Teaspoon |
| Hollow Palace boss | Angela’s Wedding Ring |

Check a fresh treasure route, then inspect the relic and confirm pickup. Saved chest offers and class forge rewards honor their existing commitments.

## Boss fights

Fight a chapter boss past half health and compare its windup and recovery with the start of the fight. The low-health pace should be quicker while warnings stay readable. Save through an interruption and check that the same attack resumes.

Take Bloomheart below 35% to reach its new final phase. The first clear is best experienced without reading the encounter script. Test interruption recovery during the new phase after you have seen it once.
