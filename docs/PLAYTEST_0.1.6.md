# Garden: Bad Bloom 0.1.6 player checks

The rest-room forge now has a recipe for each heroine. Start a fresh run to check the new chapter rewards. A resumed run keeps any boss offer that was already saved on an open chest.

## First two recipes

The [0.1.5 checks](PLAYTEST_0.1.5.md) cover Rosewall Execution, Elegy Spiral, and the forge’s back, resume, and confirmation flow. Those checks also apply to this package.

## Nixia

- Take **Orchid Punch Card** from the Mushroom Kingdom boss chest and **Metronome Heart** from the Clockwork Conservatory boss chest. Forge **Orchid Overclock** at a later rest room.
- Every eighth landed basic attack should release one close burst. A landed punch when the beat ring glows gold should bank one Momentum for the next paid skill.
- With the gauntlets equipped, land three punches on consecutive beats to prime a faster four-hit **Security Breach**. Restart once after priming it or during the cut-in. Check that the faster rhythm and finishing launch fire once.

## Virelia

- Equip **Snarewood Bow** from a Mushroom Kingdom treasure chest. Learn **Sewing Thread** after taking the Mushroom boss reward and walking through its exit. Forge **Tidy Disaster** at a later rest room.
- Hold a movement direction while firing to choose the wall. From a clear corner, fire at two different sides to link them. A barrel between the pins should prevent the link. Two close Tidy Arrangement placements can make a second line.
- A crossing enemy should trigger one snare and one arrow sweep. Restart with a pin or unspent wire on the floor and try the crossing after continuing.

## Zaria

- Take **Glitter Crime** from the Mushroom Kingdom boss chest and **Painted Exit** from the Clockwork Conservatory boss chest. Forge **Colorstorm Path** at a later rest room.
- Flank an enemy that has turned to attack and check for a stronger hit and a paint patch. Stepping onto a patch should spend it once.
- With Colorstorm Path equipped, use **Graffiti Cut** to leave a return mark. Land three basic attacks to leave paint. Turn toward the return mark and Dodge while the paint is still live. A clear return should spend the mark and release one storm where Zaria lands. Try returning with cover across the line to check ordinary dodge behavior.
- Restart after placing the mark or while the return dodge is in progress. Check that the saved return and storm resolve once.
