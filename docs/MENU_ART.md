## Fine pixel sampling

The artwork is composed on the fixed 864 × 390 viewport. Portrait, eye-crop, and ability-icon source and destination bounds are aligned to whole logical pixels. Nearest sampling keeps portraits, scenery, effects, and UI on the same fine grid when the view is enlarged.

Godot window captures were checked at both 864 × 390 and 1080 × 488.
