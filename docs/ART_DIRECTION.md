# Art direction

The owner's concept board guides the floral conservatory, cream and brass interface, battle readability and the five heroines' likenesses. The design bible and the owner's orientation decision govern the playable layout.

The built-in image generation tool produced these project assets. Sprite atlases retain the original alpha channel. The engine selects source regions and animates the anchored sprites during play.
